What we do

We synthesise

We read the official portals each week and turn them into plain English, maps and charts.

We signpost

Every figure links to a named public source and date. We point you to the authorities, never replace them.

We stay calm

Context over clickbait. We state uncertainty and correct mistakes promptly.

Who it is for

Anyone who needs a clear, sourced view of animal disease without reading every portal.

  • Farmers and smallholders

    What is spreading and where, with links to the official guidance.

  • Vets and students

    Sourced numbers and maps you can check, cite and teach from.

  • Traders and industry

    Trends across regions and species, linked to the official notices.

  • Journalists and educators

    Plain-English context and a named source for every figure.
