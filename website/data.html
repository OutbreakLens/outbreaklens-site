How it is made

  • Each source is read as published. Nothing is estimated or filled in.
  • When two sources describe the same event, both rows are kept and linked in the same_as column, so every figure stays traceable.
  • Places are given at country, region or county level. Points are the centre of that area and never show a farm or other premises.
  • Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Countries use ISO 3166 three-letter codes.
